# Polish 1.9.2 — design

Approved by Mark on 2026-11-26. Four small fixes left open after 1.9.0. No new features, no project-file format change.

## Goals

1. **Timeline bars never overlap.** Today a bar's row is `layer % 3`, so two drawings on one frame often share a row. Rows are assigned so bars that overlap in time never share a row.
2. **Undo covers slow-motion regions.** Adding, moving, resizing, deleting or re-speeding a region is one undo step in the same history as drawings. Cmd+Z undoes whichever came last.
3. **Even frame steps around a freeze inside a sped-up region.** A freeze splits a speed region into two play segments; each picks its source frames from its own start, so the step across the freeze can be a frame long or short. Both halves pick frames from one shared grid.
4. **Callouts: edit the text, wrap long labels.** Double-clicking a callout reopens its label for typing in place; the callout is rebuilt around the same dot. On finishing (first typing or an edit), a line wider than 40% of the picture width is broken at word boundaries.

## Non-goals

- L shuttle skipping the freeze preview; legacy late freeze doubling with an auto freeze.
- Live wrapping while typing (would need `fabric.Textbox` and a format change).
- Any change to exports that have no hold inside a speed region.

## Anti-goals

- A project saved by 1.9.1 looking different in 1.9.2, other than timeline rows.
- An export without a freeze-in-speed-region changing by even one frame.

## Approaches

- **Rows:** greedy interval packing (chosen) over keeping `layer % 3` with offsets; packing is the only one that guarantees no overlap.
- **Undo:** one shared history (chosen) over a separate stack for regions; two stacks make Cmd+Z ambiguous.
- **Frame grid:** carry a phase into the second half's frame rule (chosen) over moving the split point; the freeze frame is fixed by the drawing and cannot move.
- **Callout wrap:** insert line breaks on finish (chosen) over `fabric.Textbox`; no format change, exports unchanged.

## Behaviour details

- Rows: lowest free row first, in start-time order; at least 3 rows shown; the lane grows to at most 6 rows; a bar keeps its row while it is being dragged.
- Undo: a drag on the speed lane, one hold-`W` recording, a chip click and a delete are each one step. Loading a project clears history as today.
- Callout edit: works from the Select tool and the Callout tool. Colour, width and style come from the callout itself, not the toolbar. Timing, id and layer are kept. Emptying the text deletes the callout as one undo step. An edit that changes nothing leaves no undo step.
- Wrap: a single word wider than the limit is not broken. Typed line breaks are kept.

## Testing

Focused tests per fix while building. Item 3 is verified against real ffmpeg, frame by frame. One full suite run and one in-app plus MP4/GIF pass before release.
